Certifications Explained

What our certifications mean for you.

MSME Certified

Registered and certified under the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises of India. This ensures that Indo Route Export is a formally recognized business entity, meeting all government regulatory requirements for trade operations.

APEDA Certified

Certified by the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority. This certifies our compliance with national export standards, authorizing us to ship agricultural commodities globally.

FSSAI Certified

Licensed and certified by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. This ensures that every agricultural product we export complies fully with safety, hygiene, and international quality guidelines.
